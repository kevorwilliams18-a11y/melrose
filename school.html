Our story

Our History

Melrose Primary & Infant School was founded in 1964 under Principal Isaiah Jackson, with eighteen teachers and 930 students. Successive boards, dedicated staff and engaged parents have helped shape a school committed to high academic and moral standards.

“Only the Best is Good Enough.”

Our Vision & Mission Statement

Our Vision

“Promoting a better quality of life through discipline, hard work, and creativity.”

Our Mission

We are committed to uplifting the young people of our community through academic, social, spiritual, moral, cultural and practical activities, equipping each student to fulfil their potential and contribute to the community and nation.

Our Philosophy

Education develops the spirit, mind, body and character. Our qualified, caring staff support holistic development in an environment that cultivates excellence.

A welcome from our Principal

Ms. Keisha Waller welcomes new and returning families to a school where every child should feel safe, supported and inspired to excel. Student success is a shared responsibility, built on open communication and partnership between home and school.

What guides us

Our R.I.S.E. values

R — Respect

We treat every person with dignity, value every voice and take responsibility for our actions.

I — Integrity

We act with honesty, transparency and professionalism. Our actions are consistent with our words.

S — Spirituality

We believe in something greater than ourselves and cultivate love, compassion and kindness.

E — Excellence

We set high expectations, support every student in meeting them and commit to continuous improvement.

Inclusive learning and support

Our Special Education Unit and Sensory Room support students with special needs within the school’s available provision. Placement requires an official Ministry referral or valid psycho-educational assessment and an agreed Individual Intervention Plan, reviewed each term.

Contact the school office to discuss assessment, placement and available support for your child.

Families as partners

We welcome parents and guardians as partners in their child’s education. Support learning and homework, encourage punctual attendance, model the R.I.S.E. values at home and take part in school events.

Visitors should report to the main office, sign the Visitor’s Register and wear a visitor’s pass. Classrooms and restricted areas may only be entered with authorisation.
