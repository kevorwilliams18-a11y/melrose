Our School Leadership

Our Board of Management

Meet the people supporting our school’s leadership, families and community.

Ms. Chrystal Harewood

Chairwoman

Guided by our motto, R.I.S.E., Melrose Primary, Junior High & Infant School is dedicated to inspiring excellence, strength of character, and continuous growth. Together, we empower every child to reach higher and achieve their full potential and succeed.

Mrs. Thelma Broderick

Vice Chairwoman

Every child deserves a school community that encourages learning, kindness and confidence. By supporting our teachers and strengthening the partnership between school and home, we can help each learner grow, overcome challenges and work towards their full potential.

Mrs. Keisha Waller

Principal

At Melrose Primary, Junior High & Infant School, our children are at the centre of all we do. We are committed to nurturing their learning, character and confidence in a caring environment. Together with our families and community, we encourage every child to strive for excellence and take pride in their achievements.

Ms. Elizabeth Miller

Vice Principal

A positive school experience is built through encouragement, consistent guidance and teamwork. We support our students in developing good learning habits, showing respect and taking responsibility. Working closely with teachers and families, we help every child feel supported and ready to learn.

Mrs. Ann-Marie White

PTA President

At Melrose Primary, Junior High & Infant School, every child benefits when parents and teachers work together. As PTA President, I encourage families to stay involved, share their ideas and support the school’s activities.

Through open communication, mutual respect and a shared commitment to our children, we can build a caring school community where every child feels valued and encouraged to succeed.

Ms. Paula Brown

Academic Staff Representative

Our teachers share a commitment to helping children learn, ask questions and discover their strengths. As Academic Staff Representative, I support open communication and collaboration so that classroom experiences remain engaging, caring and focused on our students’ growth.

Trevor E. S. Smith

Community Representative

At Melrose, we encourage children to pause before reacting: Stop, calm down and think, then choose wisely. A few deep breaths and ten quiet seconds can prevent conflict. Speak calmly, walk away or seek help from a trusted adult. Parents, teachers and community members must model the same respect and self-control.

We may not control what happens to us, but we can control how we respond.
