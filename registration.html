Online application

Register your child

Complete the registration application with your child’s details, parent or guardian contact information, and document checklist.

Start registration →

The form opens in a new tab. Contact melrose.primary.saw@moey.gov.jm for assistance.

Submitting an application does not confirm admission. Upload supporting documents in the application where requested. Contact the school office for assistance and payment arrangements.

Documents and fees

  • Parent/guardian ID and TRN
  • Child’s birth certificate and immunization card
  • Two passport-size photographs of the child
  • One passport-size photograph of the driver, if applicable
  • Assessment report, if applicable

Registration fee: $1,000.00
School contribution: $10,000.00 (includes P.E. gear)
